THE BACKSTORY

Made over
a weekend.

Nuggetheim was made over a weekend with the help of a friend during my freshman-year summer in high school.

It wasn't some huge planned-out game project. We just had an idea, started making it, and kept going until we had something we could actually play.

That is probably the most important part of the project to me: I actually made the thing.

WHERE IT IS NOW

Paused.
Not erased.

Nuggetheim is unfinished, and I'm not planning to pursue it right now. My attention is on college applications, school, music, YouTube, and the other stuff happening in my life.

I'm keeping it on this site anyway because it is part of what I've made. Maybe I'll come back to it someday. Maybe I won't. Either way, the weekend happened.
